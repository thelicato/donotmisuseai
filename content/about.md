## About This Manifesto

The Do Not Misuse AI manifesto is a human-generated document outlining the domains where artificial intelligence should not be deployed. It serves as a reference for policymakers, developers, and the public to understand the ethical boundaries of AI use.

## Why This Exists

As AI capabilities grow rapidly, the pressure to apply AI to every domain intensifies. This rush often overlooks fundamental rights, human dignity, and the importance of human judgment. This manifesto exists to draw clear lines.

## Contributing

This is a living document. Categories and principles will evolve as technology and society change. Contributions are welcome via [GitHub](https://github.com/thelicato/donotmisuseai).
