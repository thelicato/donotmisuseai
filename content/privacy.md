## Privacy Policy

This website does not use analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts of any kind. No additional data is collected, stored, or processed beyond what the hosting provider requires to operate the service.

The site is hosted on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com). Netlify's edge network automatically logs standard web request data (e.g., IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp) for operational purposes such as security and performance monitoring. These logs are retained temporarily and are not used for tracking or profiling.

No personal data is shared with third parties.
